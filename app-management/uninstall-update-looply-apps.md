# Uninstall/Update Looply Apps

Looply apps can be uninstalled or updated across organisation from the **User Manager** tab.&#x20;

* Go to **App Manager** from the side navigation menu
* Click on the App tile to manage uninstallation/update
* Go to **User Manager** tab
* Search for the user using their email address
* Click Uninstall/Force update&#x20;
